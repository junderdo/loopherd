# D90

## Decision

`loopherd view`'s selected row keeps the palette's accent as its background
and takes a dark foreground in every palette, amending D86:

| Palette | Truecolor | Contrast | 256-color | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| purple-orange | (20,16,28) on (255,154,31) | 8.83 | 232 on 208 | 8.31 |
| phosphor | #0c0f0b on #ffd230 | 13.33 | 233 on 221 | 13.50 |
| catppuccin | #1e1e2e (Mocha base) on #fab387 | 9.27 | 234 on 216 | 9.52 |

Each 256-color fallback is the grayscale-ramp index (232–255) whose contrast
is nearest the truecolor pair's. The ramp is fixed: base16 and tinted-shell
themes remap 16–21, and index 16 there is an orange that would read at
1.02:1 on 208. A test pins every pair at 4.5:1 or more.

## Why

Chosen by the human on LH-288 (2026-11-19). The light text on the accent
read at 1.22 to 1.79:1, well under WCAG's 4.5:1.
