# D74

## Decision

Moving a member's pane out of its tab within its workspace leaves no
unretracted `gone`, whichever of the `pane.moved` and `tab.closed` hooks
runs first (D66).

- A `gone` the `tab.closed` hook records carries the closed tab:
  `{"kind":"gone","tab":"w1:t1"}`. Other `gone` entries carry none.
- The `tab.closed` hook re-reads the member's ledger entry while it holds
  the member's mailbox lock, and records nothing once the entry no longer
  holds the closed tab.
- The `pane.moved` hook, after it follows the move in the ledger (D73),
  takes the mailbox lock and appends a `moved` entry when the last `gone`
  or `moved` there is a `gone` carrying the tab the pane left, even with a
  `stop` or `blocked` written since: the pane is live. That `moved`
  retracts it.
- A `gone` is retracted when the next `gone` or `moved` after it is a
  `moved`. A watch prints no line for a retracted `gone`, nor for the
  `moved`, the view's ticker leaves both out, and a watch's poll doesn't count a retracted `gone` as already
  recorded. A `moved` is a sign of life, so the member's next `gone` is
  recorded. `reports` lists `moved` like `gone`.

The move hook writes the ledger before it locks the mailbox, and the close
hook reads the ledger under that lock, so in any interleaving of the two
hooks, and whatever else writes the mailbox between them, either the close
hook sees the new tab and records nothing, or the move hook sees the `gone`
and retracts it.

Limits:

- A watch that polls between the two hooks prints the `gone` before its
  `moved` lands; the `moved` prints nothing.
- A move whose hook fails, or is dropped past herdr's 32 running plugin
  commands, retracts nothing.

LH-266 settled it, dropping D73's limit on a move racing its old tab's
close.

## Why

herdr fires `tab.closed` right after `pane.moved` when the move empties the
tab, and every member starts alone in its own tab, so the race was the usual
move. The close event names only the tab, so a close hook that runs first
cannot know a move is coming; only the move hook can undo what it did. An
appended `moved` keeps the mailbox append-only, which the readers' cursors
rely on, where removing the `gone` would not. Tagging the `gone` with its tab
keeps a move from retracting a `gone` the pane itself recorded.
