# D156

Amends D143.

## Decision

A lead's gate request waits like its question. D143's wait covers both.

- **A kind of its own.** `loopherd report --kind gate <text>` files a gate
  request: a lead whose shepherd runs its gates asks with it, not a note.
  Like a note it doesn't count as stopping on a question, sends no
  `question` notification, and labels no pane. `watch` and plain `reports`
  print it once as `<lead> gate <time> <text>`.
- **It waits.** The broker writes D143's `.awaiting-<pane>` mark for a
  `gate` as for a `question`, and the same prompt answers both. A lead is
  waiting from its oldest `question` or `gate` after its last `answered` or
  `start`.
- **Status names the oldest.** `loopherd status` and the view show `gate
  waiting <age>` on the lead's row, or `question waiting <age>` when the
  oldest waiting entry is a question, after its status (`idle, gate waiting
  18m`). The age is in whole minutes, as `stalled` is. `--json` adds
  `waiting: {kind, minutes}` to the lead.
- **Unchanged.** D143's 30-minute `waiting` notification and shepherd's watch
  line (LH-496) cover gate waits once they land, since they read the same
  wait. A reply is still not matched to one request.

LH-553 settled it, folding in LH-494.

## Why

On 2026-11-24 between 23:22 and 00:02 UTC the shepherd's gates for #424 and
#429 never started, and four workers sat idle behind them for 20 to 40
minutes. A lead filed its gate requests as notes, which D143's wait doesn't
read, so nothing in `status`, the view or either watch showed the stall.

- **A kind over a note's text:** matching `Gate request:` in a note would
  make a note's wording load-bearing; a kind is checked by the CLI.
- **A kind over a question:** a gate request isn't for the human, so it
  mustn't notify as a question, label the pane, or count as the lead
  stopping on one.
