# D76

## Decision

A single move of a member's pane out of a tab or workspace it emptied
leaves no unretracted `gone`, whichever of the `pane.moved`, `tab.closed`
and `workspace.closed` hooks runs first (D66). This covers moves within a
workspace (D73) and to another one (D74).

- A `gone` the `tab.closed` hook records carries the closed tab, and one
  the `workspace.closed` hook records the closed workspace:
  `{"kind":"gone","tab":"w1:t1"}`, `{"kind":"gone","workspace":"w1"}`.
  Other `gone` entries carry neither.
- Each close hook re-reads the member's ledger entry while it holds the
  member's mailbox lock, and records nothing once the entry no longer holds
  the closed tab or workspace.
- The `pane.moved` hook, after it follows the move in the ledger, takes the
  mailbox lock and appends a `moved` entry when the last `gone` or `moved`
  there is a `gone` carrying the tab or workspace the pane left, even with
  a `stop` or `blocked` written since: the pane is live. That `moved`
  retracts it.
- A `gone` is retracted when the next `gone` or `moved` after it is a
  `moved`. A watch prints no line for a retracted `gone`, nor for the
  `moved`, the view's ticker leaves both out, and a watch's poll doesn't
  count a retracted `gone` as already recorded. A `moved` is a sign of
  life, so the member's next `gone` is recorded. `reports --all` lists
  `moved` like `gone`.

The move hook writes the ledger before it locks the mailbox, and the close
hooks read the ledger under that lock, so in any interleaving of the hooks,
and whatever else writes the mailbox between them, either a close hook sees
the member gone from what closed and records nothing, or the move hook sees
its `gone` and retracts it. A move that empties a workspace fires both
closes; the second finds the first's `gone`, or the move's ledger write,
and records nothing more.

Limits:

- A watch that polls between the hooks prints the `gone` before its
  `moved` lands; the `moved` prints nothing.
- A move whose hook fails, or is dropped past herdr's 32 running plugin
  commands, retracts nothing.
- Two quick moves whose hooks run in reverse order can still leave a false
  `gone`: the second move is skipped (D73's two-move limit), the first
  points the ledger at the tab the second emptied, and that tab's close
  then records a `gone` no move retracts.
- A real exit racing the move can be retracted: when the close hook's
  `gone` lands first, the `pane.exited` one is skipped as already recorded,
  and the move's `moved` then retracts the only `gone`. A watch's two-poll
  check still records it.
- The new tab's close read against a ledger the move hook hasn't written
  yet finds no member and records nothing; a watch's poll still covers it.
- A loopherd older than this, after a rollback or as a `loopherd view`
  still running from before an install, reads a `moved` line as a bad
  line: it warns, `doctor`'s mailbox check fails, and the retracted `gone`
  prints as real.

LH-266 settled it, dropping D73's and D74's limits on a move racing the
close of what it left. It amends D66's once-per-start rule, where a
`moved` now counts as a sign of life, and its watch poll, which no longer
counts a retracted `gone`, and D69's close hooks, which now tag their
`gone` and re-read the ledger.

## Why

herdr fires `tab.closed`, and `workspace.closed` when the workspace empties
too, right after `pane.moved`, and every member starts alone in its own
tab, so the race was the usual move. A close event names only the tab or
workspace, so a close hook that runs first cannot know a move is coming;
only the move hook can undo what it did. An appended `moved` keeps the
mailbox append-only, which the readers' cursors rely on, where removing the
`gone` would not. Tagging the `gone` with what closed keeps a move from
retracting a `gone` the pane itself recorded.
