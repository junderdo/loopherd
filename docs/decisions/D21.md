# D21

## Decision

The stop hook, `loopherd report` and a lead's `watch` match a non-retired
ledger entry on pane id and herdr socket together. An entry recorded without
a socket matches only a caller that has no `HERDR_SOCKET_PATH` either; it no
longer matches its pane id in any herdr. This narrows D16's pane-only
fallback. The SessionStart hook already required an exact socket and is
unchanged.

## Why

Pane ids repeat across herdr sessions. Under the fallback, a Claude in a
second herdr that got a socketless member's pane id had its stops filed in
that member's mailbox, and the shepherd's watch showed them as that member's
(LH-95). D20 already kept such entries out of handoffs and `restart`; this
removes the misattribution at the match itself. Every spawn and revive
records the socket, so only old entries lose the fallback. Migrating them
is out of scope; reviving one records its socket.
