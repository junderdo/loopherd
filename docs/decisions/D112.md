# D112

## Decision

A side session the shepherd starts for the human, tied to a card (a grill,
a spike, a hand-run prototype), is a member of the card's herd, started from
a template marked `attended = true`. loopherd tracks it only so that its
merge reaches a watch; the human, who is in its pane, handles everything
else. Grilled with the human on LH-345 (2026-11-20); amends D25's "grilling
stays the human's", D107's "side sessions run as the human's own panes"
(they stay unsandboxed and as the human), and D14's slot rule.

- **Started by `add` only.** The shepherd runs `loopherd add <herd>
  <template> <params…>`, which makes the tab, worktree, branch and agent as
  for any member, from the template's `prompt` or `prompt_file`. A `source`
  line routing a task to an attended template is a bad line (D14), so feed
  never spawns one; `loopherd herds` flags an attended template listed in
  `[queue] templates`. A lead's broker refuses `add` of an attended
  template (D98, D110): only the shepherd or the human starts one.
- **`add` takes the human there.** Once an attended member's agent is up
  and prompted, `add` focuses its pane (`herdr agent focus`), switching tab
  and workspace; `--no-focus` opts out. A start that fails or comes up
  `blocked` leaves focus where it was. So when the human asks the shepherd
  to grill a card, the shepherd runs `add` and the human lands in the grill.
- **And brings them back.** `add` records the caller's `HERDR_PANE_ID` on
  the member as `return_pane` (none when run outside herdr), and fills a
  `{shepherd_pane}` placeholder with it. When the conversation ends (cards
  approved, PR open), the grill tells the human and focuses that pane,
  staying open in its tab until the merge. At the reap, `retire --close`
  focuses `return_pane` only if the member's pane is focused then.
- **Its PR is found by branch,** through the template's `merged` check, and
  its close-out is the herd's: feed's `after_merge` once `merged` passes,
  then its `done` check reaps it with `retire --close`, closing the pane,
  worktree and branch, even if the human is still reading there.
- **The watch sees only `merged` and `gone`.** The watch that reads the
  herd's workers (the lead's, or the shepherd's for an unled herd) prints
  `<name> merged` and `<name> gone` for it, as for a worker, and nothing for
  its stops, questions, `blocked` dialogs or handoffs. It sends nothing
  through `[notify]`, a `merge_alert` included, and holds off no
  `drained` line (D140). A `gone` after its reap prints nothing, since a watch
  reads only unretired members.
- **Hands off for the lead.** A lead's broker refuses `tell`, `prompt`,
  `restart`, `retire` and `release` on an attended member, and the lead's
  `blocked_dialogs` switch never applies to it. The lead acts only after the
  merge: card to Done, then feed.
- **Not a worker in the counts.** It holds no `max` slot, has no context
  limit or handoff wake, and is left out of `stats`, as leads are (D30).
  `status` and `view` mark it `attended`.
- **Revive.** Startup and nameless `revive` skip an attended member whose
  last mailbox entry is a `gone` from closing its pane, tab or workspace,
  and list it as skipped; one herdr lost is revived (D140). `revive --name`
  brings it back.
- **The grill's own close-out shrinks.** LH-344's flow ships first as is.
  Once grills run attended, the grill moves its card to Done, runs
  `scripts/trello unblock` and pings the shepherd; it no longer removes its
  worktree or branch, which the reap does. The ping stays as the fast path.
- **Which sessions.** Side sessions tied to a card. One with no card has no
  PR to watch and stays a stray.

Built as LH-351 (the flag), LH-352 (the lead's refusals) and LH-353 (grills
in `loopherd-cards`, after LH-344).

## Why

LH-284's grill (D107, PR 302) ran outside the ledger; the human merged its
PR and closed the pane before its close-out, nothing told the shepherd, and
the card sat in Backlog until the human mentioned it. The self-close-out
LH-344 adds fails the same way whenever the pane closes first.

- **A template flag, not a ledger kind.** A template already carries the
  branch, the `merged` and `done` checks, `after_merge` and the reap. A
  herd-less `loopherd side <card>` would need its own way to find a PR,
  putting GitHub knowledge into loopherd, which D9 and D14 keep in the herd
  file.
- **Only `merged` and `gone`.** The human is in the pane, so its stops,
  questions and dialogs are already in front of them; a grill stops every
  few seconds and would wake the lead on each.
- **The lead's watch, in a led herd.** It already runs members' `merged`
  checks and owns the post-merge steps; the shepherd's watch runs no member
  checks in a led herd (D72). The steps are no-ops when the grill has
  already closed out.
- **No slot, no stats.** `max` bounds what the herd produces for review; a
  grill is the human's own time and shouldn't stall the queue. Its cycle is
  the conversation's length, which says nothing about trusting leads (D30).
- **Focus in `add`, not the runbook.** The flag already means the human is
  in that pane, and `add` holds the pane it recorded, so the move can't be
  forgotten or land on the wrong pane. The way back is the caller's own
  pane, not the herdr agent named `shepherd`, which survives a rename. The
  reap moves focus only off a pane the human is looking at, since a merge
  can land hours later while they work elsewhere.
- **No revive of a closed grill.** A pane the human closed before the merge
  is one they were done with; a herdr restart shouldn't reopen it.
