# D71

## Decision

The herdr plugin hooks `pane.moved` to `loopherd herdr-event-hook`. The
hook finds the non-retired member on `HERDR_SOCKET_PATH` whose `pane_id` is
the event's `data.previous_pane_id`, and sets its ledger entry's `pane_id`,
`tab_id` and `workspace` to `data.pane.pane_id`, `data.pane.tab_id` and
`data.pane.workspace_id`, under the ledger lock. An event missing any of
these, or naming no member, changes nothing. The hook still never calls
herdr. Closing the member's old tab or workspace then records no `gone`,
and closing its new one does (D69).

The [payload](../research/herdr-events.md#panemoved) was probed on the dev
herdr over a raw socket subscription, without linking the plugin (D62).

Limits:

- A move to another workspace gives the pane a new id. The member's agent
  keeps the old one in `HERDR_PANE_ID`, so its `loopherd report`, Stop hook
  and SessionStart hook no longer find it in the ledger until a revive or
  restart.
- Moving a member's pane out of a tab or workspace it was alone in fires
  `pane.moved`, then `tab.closed` (and `workspace.closed`) for the one it
  left. herdr runs hooks unordered (D66), so when the close's hook reads the
  ledger before the move's hook writes it, the member gets a false `gone`.
  Every member starts alone in its own tab, so this is the usual move.
- `workspace_label` is left as it was, so a revive after a move to another
  workspace starts the member back in the workspace it was spawned in.

LH-257 settled it.

## Why

`herdr pane move` changes a pane's tab, and on a move between workspaces
its id, while the agent keeps running. Following the move from the event's
own payload keeps the ledger's ids, which `tab.closed`, `workspace.closed`,
`pane.closed` and every watch look members up by, true without a herdr call
in a hook that must stay fast and offline.
