# D71

## Decision

`loopherd restart` of a lead resolves its six switches and `review_rounds`
from the herd file and `[lead_defaults]` at restart time, as `loopherd lead`
does, and its resume prompt carries them: `Your switches: merge on, …,
blocked_dialogs off. Your review-round limit: <n>.` A lead whose herd file or
`config.toml` can't be read, or that has no herd or `[lead]`, is refused
before the `/clear`, even with `--force`, so nothing is lost. Such a lead
could be restarted before this.

Amended on LH-264: the resume prompt also opens with the lead's whole
`prompt` or `prompt_file`, re-read and rendered at restart with its
placeholders filled as `loopherd lead` fills them, ahead of the
read-your-handoff line. A `prompt_file` that can't be read refuses the
restart before the `/clear` too.

## Why

The `/clear` drops the lead's original prompt, which held `{switches}` and
`{review_rounds}`, so a successor fell back to the skill's "3 when unset" and
forgot the switches the tool hook doesn't enforce (`new_cards`,
`design_escalation`, `blocked_dialogs`). Resolving at restart matches what
the hook enforces now. The human chose the resume prompt over telling the
lead to read `loopherd herds`, so the lead's context holds them without a
step it might skip. Decided on LH-258.

The `/clear` drops the rest of that prompt as well: the project facts a
`prompt_file` carries, such as the gh account form the skill's Lead section
points to and the gate command. A successor without them asks the human to
merge with a bare `gh pr merge` as the wrong account. The human chose
re-rendering the whole prompt into the resume prompt over a file the lead is
told to read, for the same reason as the switches.
