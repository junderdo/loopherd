# D99

## Decision

Amends D11 (LH-302). From this version on, loopherd loads a ledger written
by a later version, and saves it back without losing what it doesn't
understand. Versions from before this one still fail on an unknown variant.

- **Unknown fields** on an `AgentRecord`, its `earlier` runs included, land
  in a catch-all map and are written back unchanged.
- **Unknown enum variants** (`Outcome`, `AfterMerge`, `Role`) load as an
  `Unknown` variant holding the raw JSON, and are written back unchanged.
  An `AfterMerge` payload with a field this version lacks is unknown too,
  kept whole.
- **Behaviour on an unknown variant** is the cautious one:
  - `stats` shows an unknown outcome as `-` and counts it `unrecorded`; its
    `--json` carries the raw value.
  - `feed` leaves a member with an unknown `after_merge` alone (no checks,
    no retire, no commands), and `watch` keeps its slot taken, as for a
    pending one.
  - An unknown role may be a lead, so every guard that refuses or protects
    a lead covers it: the tool hook and feed's after_merge gate apply a
    lead's switches to it, `retire` refuses it retiring itself, `release`
    refuses it, `lead` refuses a second lead beside it, a lead's broker
    refuses to reach it, and `feed` and `watch` leave it alone instead of
    checking or retiring it.
  - Elsewhere it counts as a worker, the cautious side there: it holds a
    queue slot and keeps its herd from reading out of work, and lead
    bookkeeping (doctor's sandbox and broker checks, the watch notes
    `pause` and `release` clear, a lead's hold on out-of-work) passes it by.

Rules for future changes to the ledger:

- A new field is optional: `#[serde(default)]`, and `skip_serializing_if`
  when its absence means the default.
- A new enum stored in the ledger ends with an `#[serde(untagged)]
  Unknown(serde_json::Value)` variant, takes `deny_unknown_fields` when it
  has struct payloads, and code matching it decides what an unknown value
  means rather than failing.
- A new struct nested in a record carries its own `#[serde(flatten)]`
  catch-all. `Process` is frozen instead: it is a pid and start time
  compared by value, so new process identity goes in a new field on the
  record.
- Never rename or repurpose a field or a variant's tag; add a new one.

## Why

Mixed versions are normal: the installed binary lags a merge until
`scripts/install` runs, and members run their worktree's build. Before
this, a variant one binary didn't know failed the whole ledger parse,
breaking every command and the Stop and session hooks, and every typed
load and save by an older binary silently dropped the newer fields. Keeping
the raw value, rather than a bare `Unknown` tag, means a round trip through
an older binary doesn't erase what a newer one recorded. An unknown role
fails closed because the lead guards are what keep a lead from a merge,
install or rebase its human hasn't allowed, from retiring itself out of its
gates, and from being reaped or released as a worker.
