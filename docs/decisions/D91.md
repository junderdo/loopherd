# D91

## Decision

With `[shepherd] self_restart = true` in `config.toml` (default false), the
shepherd restarts itself after its handoff with no human input. It reuses
D82's `shepherd.json` record and SessionStart resume, and D78's waiter and
locks. The switch is the only setting; every time below is fixed.

- **Start.** In its handoff turn, after writing `handoffs/shepherd.md`, the
  shepherd runs `loopherd restart --self` from its own pane and ends its
  turn. `--self` takes the caller as the shepherd when its pane, socket and
  Claude process match `shepherd.json`, and starts a detached waiter. Its
  last message warns the human: it restarts itself in 5 minutes, and any
  prompt in this pane stops that.
- **Refusals**, each telling why and leaving the handoff for the manual
  `/clear` and `go`: the switch is off; the UserPromptSubmit hook isn't
  installed (cancelling depends on it); or the last self-restart ran
  within 30 minutes, which also sends a `self_restart` notification.
- **Turn end.** The Stop hook records the shepherd's stops in
  `shepherd.json` when pane, socket and Claude process match, read as
  `finished` or `paused` the way members' are. The waiter needs `finished`
  with nothing in flight since the handoff, and herdr showing the pane
  idle or done.
- **The human first.** It also needs the pane's input box empty (read from
  its prompt line) and no prompt from the human in that pane for 5 minutes.
  Those 5 minutes are the warning window.
- **Cancel.** A prompt in the shepherd's pane while a waiter is pending
  makes the UserPromptSubmit hook mark the restart cancelled in
  `shepherd.json`, stop the waiter, and add context telling the shepherd to
  tell the human to `/clear` and send `go`, or to ask whether to re-arm. A
  yes re-runs `restart --self`, which starts a new 5-minute window. A
  cancelled restart doesn't count toward the 30-minute guard, and a waiter
  never re-arms itself.
- **Restart.** The waiter sends `/clear`, waits up to 30 seconds for D82's
  resume to mark the record used, then sends `go`; if the record isn't
  marked used by then, it sends D82's fallback line (`continue as the
  loopherd shepherd from <path>`) instead. The resumed shepherd's first
  message says it restarted itself; success sends no notification.
- **Give up** after 30 minutes without its conditions holding, when the
  pane or Claude process is gone, or on any failure: write the reason to
  `shepherd.json`, send a new `[notify]` kind `self_restart` (in the
  default `kinds`) and label the shepherd's pane `shepherd: self-restart
  gave up: <why>`. The handoff stays for the manual resume.
- **Doctor** warns when the switch is on and the UserPromptSubmit hook is
  missing, or `[notify]` is off, since a give-up would then go unseen.
- **Skill.** "Your own handoff" step 3 runs `restart --self` when the
  switch is on and gives the warning; otherwise it is unchanged.

## Why

Grilled on LH-277 (2026-11-19), the automatic restart D82 deferred. An
overnight shepherd should keep relaying lead questions after a handoff
instead of waiting for the human's `/clear`. Against it: a `/clear` the
human didn't type wipes the screen they read the shepherd on, and herdr's
typing replaces a half-typed input. Opt-in, the idle window and the
cancel-by-typing answer that; a restart that can't happen safely falls back
to the manual one and says so.

Rejected:

- **The watch starting the waiter** on `shepherd handoff`: it fires before
  the handoff is written.
- **herdr's idle status alone** as the turn end: it reads idle with a
  background shell still running, which a `/clear` would kill.
- **Waiting for unanswered relayed questions**: they survive the `/clear`
  in the handoff and mailbox, and gating on them would stop the overnight
  case it exists for.
- **Re-arming after a cancel by itself**: the human is present, so it
  could fire while they read.
- **Configurable times**: one switch until a time proves wrong.
