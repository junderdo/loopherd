# D30

## Decision

`loopherd stats` reads only the ledger and the mailboxes. A retired worker's
row runs from its ledger `created` (started) to its `retired_at`: time to its
first `done` report, the number of `done` reports (review rounds), time from
the last `done` to retirement (waiting on merge), and the total. Only `done`
reports between `created` and `retired_at` count, so a reused name's earlier
mailbox entries don't. A value that can't be computed (no `done`, no
`retired_at` in an old ledger) shows as `-` and is left out of the herd's
medians and maxima. Leads and entries with no herd are left out; unretired
workers are listed apart with their time so far and aren't summarized.
`--since` keeps members started on or after the given UTC date or time.

## Why

The numbers are for judging whether leads can be trusted with more autonomy
(LH-110), which is a question about workers' cycles: how long a card takes,
how many review rounds it needs, and how long its PR waits on the human. A
lead runs for the life of its herd, so its times would swamp the medians.
Starting time is `created`, not the mailbox's first `start`, because revive
appends further `start` entries and older members may have none. Filtering
by start keeps a member's row the same whichever day it is read.
