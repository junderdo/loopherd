# D129

## Decision

Amends D103: minors and nits are gathered once, when a PR passes or its
merge question is written, not round by round (LH-346).

- **One gathering per PR.** From every round's review file, not only the
  passing one, the shepherd or lead files the minors as one follow-up
  (routed as D103 says) and posts the nits as one PR comment marked
  non-blocking.
- **The handoff records it.** The handoff names, per PR, whether its
  minors and nits are gathered, so a restarted lead or shepherd doesn't
  refile them.
- **Wording.** Later rounds don't repeat earlier rounds' minors and nits;
  that is why a failed round's minors live only in its file.

## Why

PR 303's reviews found the skill routing "every round's minors as for any
minor" with no moment named, so a lead could file per round, and a lead
restarted after filing had nothing telling it the follow-up existed.
Gathering at pass or merge question yields one follow-up per PR, and
recording it in the handoff closes the refile gap.
