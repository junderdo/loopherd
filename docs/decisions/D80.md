# D80

## Decision

A lead's watch prints `<herd> running low  0 claimable, <m> unready, no
workers open` when its herd isn't paused, claimable is 0, unready is above
0, and no (non-retired) worker is open.

- **Its own streak.** The line prints once per streak, even while D60's
  `running low` streak is live. The key names the newest `created` among
  the herd's members, retired ones included, as `out of work`'s does
  (D72), so the streak ends when a worker opens, including one that opened
  and retired while no watch ran. It also ends when claimable rises above 0
  or unready reaches 0, each appending `ended` to the fired file.
- **One line.** When the plain and the starved condition first hold on the
  same poll, only the starved line prints, and D60's streak is recorded as
  fired, so a worker opening later doesn't print the plain line.
- **Otherwise as D60.** D60's streak is unchanged. A paused herd prints
  neither and keeps both streaks; the shepherd's watch never prints it.
- **Triage.** The lead triages as for `running low`, and its question says
  the herd has no work running.

This amends D60's once-per-streak line. `out of work` (D72) still needs
unready 0.

LH-275 settled it.

## Why

On 2026-11-18 loopherd-cards said `running low` with 3 claimable and 9
unready, and the lead triaged. Claimable then stayed at or below `max` and
unready never reached 0, so D60's streak never ended; the herd drained to
no workers, 0 claimable and 11 unready, and `out of work` couldn't fire.
The lead waited and nobody was told.

Firing `running low` again reuses the lead's triage flow, and keeps `out
of work` meaning "file new work" rather than widening it to herds with
tasks the human hasn't marked ready.
