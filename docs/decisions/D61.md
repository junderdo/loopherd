# D61

## Decision

The claimable count D60 compares with `max` is every task `source` prints
that feed would take with no cap on slots: its line parses, its template
renders, and no unreleased member holds its name. It is not capped by the
herd's free slots, as the names `feed --dry-run` would spawn now are.

This amends D60's claimable count. LH-236 settled it.

## Why

Capped by free slots, a full herd always counts at most `max` claimable, so
D60's `running low` line (claimable at or below `max`, unready above 0;
amended by D116 to `low_at`, default `2 × max`) would fire whenever any task is unready, however much ready work waits. The
uncapped count says how much ready work is left, which is what the line and
`status` mean to show.
