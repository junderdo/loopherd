# D44

## Decision

Wall-clock times that `stats`, `reports` and `watch` print are labelled UTC,
not converted to local time: stats' `started` columns are headed
`started (UTC)`, and each `reports`/`watch` line prints its time as
`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC`. A stored time too short to cut prints as stored,
with no label. `stats --json` keeps RFC 3339 with `Z`.

## Why

Ledger and mailbox times are RFC 3339 UTC, and the renderers cut them to the
minute, dropping the `Z`, so a human outside UTC read them as local. The crate
has no time-zone dependency, and UTC matches `--since` ("midnight UTC") and
the JSON, so a label fixes the misreading without a new dependency.
