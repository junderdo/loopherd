# D152

## Decision

A watch says `<member> stalled  paused <n>m: <in flight>` when a member has
sat idle on a paused stop past its herd's `stalled_after` (LH-444).

- **Stalled.** The member's last mailbox entry that says anything (its
  usage and subagent entries don't) is a stop that left work in flight and
  didn't fail, and herdr shows it idle or done. The clock runs from that
  stop, or from when this watch last saw the member working or blocked,
  whichever is later. The line carries the stop's in-flight summary, as its
  `paused` line did.
- **Threshold.** A top-level `stalled_after = <minutes>` in the herd file,
  default 30; below 1 makes the file invalid. `loopherd herds` shows it on
  each herd's header line as `stalled after <n>m`.
- **Who prints it.** A lead's watch for its unattended workers, in its 60s
  round of checks; the shepherd's for leads and unattended members of
  unled herds, in its 60s round on a quiet poll. Members in no herd have no
  threshold and print nothing. A lead whose own watch is running is never
  stalled: that watch is the shell it normally waits on.
- **Once per streak.** The fired-file key is `<member> stalled since <the
  stop's time>`. A new stop or report ends the streak (`ended`), as does
  herdr showing the member working or blocked on any poll, and a member
  that stalls again prints again. herdr unreadable (every status unknown)
  neither starts nor ends a streak. A busy spell seen by an earlier watch
  is forgotten, so after a re-arm the clock runs from the stop.
- **`status`** applies the same rules (no attended members, no lead with
  its watch running) and shows a stalled herd member's status as `stalled <n>m`, and
  `status --json` gives it `stalled: <n>` minutes. `status` keeps no watch
  state, so its clock always runs from the stop.
- **Who acts.** The lead, or the shepherd, reads the pane and, if the shell
  is hung, tells the member to drop it and carry on. Nothing kills shells
  or nudges the member automatically.

## Why

On 2026-11-22 lh-341, a release blocker, stopped `paused: 1 shell` on a
background `cargo test` that hung. The watch printed `paused` once, as
D55/D72/D80's once-per-streak lines do, and a member parked on its own
shell raises no further event, so it sat for an hour until the human
asked why the PR hadn't merged.

A `paused` stop is normal while a member waits on a shell or subagent, so
the only signal is time. Keying the streak by the stop keeps a re-armed
watch quiet about a stall it already reported, while a new stop starts a
fresh one.
