# D132

## Decision

A grill runs in one of two modes, picked per card: **interactive** (one
question at a time, live with the human, as before) or **batch**, where the
grill answers every question with its own pick and reasoning, then shows the
human the whole list once to accept or override by number. Asked for by the
human on LH-380 (2026-11-21); amends issue-tracker.md's "always a human
session" and the `loopherd-cards` lead prompt's "grilling is always the
human's", and D25's and D112's routing of grills.

- **The `grill:batch` label picks batch**; a `wayfinding:grilling` card
  without it grills interactively. Only the human adds it. A
  `grill:batch` grilling card in Backlog without `need-human` or `blocked` is
  claimable without `ready-for-agent`, which grilling cards never carry.
- **Routed by `source`** (D25): the documented routed queue sends
  `grill:batch` cards to a `grill-batch` template
  (`docs/agents/grill-batch-prompt.md`), so `loopherd stats` shows batch
  grills as their own template. Interactive grills stay attended members
  (D112) and out of stats.
- **Either mode switches partway**: "batch the rest" in an interactive
  grill; `discuss <n>` pulls a question out of a batch.
- **Nothing before the answer.** A batch grill verifies facts as a live one
  does; it files, marks ready, commits and pushes nothing until the human
  answers its batch, and re-checks every pick an override touches before
  writing the decision. Silence is never a yes, whatever a lead's `merge`.
- **Unattended batch grills are ordinary members**, so a lead's feed may
  start one. It posts its batch as a card comment headed
  `Batch grill LH-<n>, round <k>`, adds `need-human`, moves the card to
  Backlog, reports a question and stops. Its `done` check passes on a card
  in Done, or in Backlog with `need-human`, so feed reaps it and it holds no
  slot while waiting. The human answers on the card; the shepherd drops
  `need-human` and runs `loopherd release grill-batch-<n>`, and feed then
  starts a fresh member, which takes the comments after the latest round as
  the answer. Each round is its own run in `stats`. An attended batch grill,
  in its own tab with the human through `add`, stays available; an attended
  grill claims its card, so feed never starts a batch grill beside it.

## Why

Interactive grills cost one round-trip per question, though the grilling
skill already gives a pick with each. Batch keeps the human deciding but
reviewing once.

- **Batch with one review over silent auto-accept:** auto-accepting writes
  decisions and cards the human never saw, which a `merge = true` lead could
  then ship.
- **A label over the shepherd's judgment at start:** a label is recorded on
  the card and a lead's `source` can read it.
- **Stop and reap over waiting:** a member waiting in the background would
  hold a slot for as long as the human takes. A stopped card under
  `need-human` is how any card already waits on the human; reaping costs
  nothing, since nothing is committed before the answer.
