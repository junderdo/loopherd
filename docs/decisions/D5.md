## Decision

Slice 1 is `loopherd spawn` (LH-9): a herd-less tracer bullet that creates an
optional worktree, a herdr tab, a named Claude agent, and its opening prompt,
and records the agent in a ledger. CLI only. herdr sits behind a trait over
its CLI. Tests use a fake, plus live tests gated on `LOOPHERD_LIVE=1`.

## Why

Proves the loop on a small, testable slice that fixes the most frequent pain
(D3). Recording agents at birth feeds revive without a background recorder.
Grilled in LH-3.
