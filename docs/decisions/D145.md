# D145

## Decision

A grill gets its background before the human does, and the shepherd learns
its card closed out whoever closes it. Amends D112's "`add` takes the human
there" for grills, and its "the ping stays as the fast path". Settled on
LH-408 from the review of LH-353.

- **Background first.** The shepherd runs `loopherd add --no-focus` for a
  grill, always sends background with `loopherd tell` (starting
  `Background for LH-<n>`, or saying there is none beyond the card), then
  focuses the grill's pane with `herdr agent focus`. The grill claims its
  card and waits for that message before its first question.
- **The lead tells the shepherd.** In a led herd, after its after-merge
  steps for an attended `grill-<n>`, the lead leaves a `note` report
  `grill-<n> merged: LH-<n> closed out`. The grill's own ping is
  best-effort.

## Why

- With `add` focusing first, the grill could ask question 1 before `tell`
  landed, and `tell` typed into the input box the human was using. Sending
  before focusing closes both; waiting on a message that always comes keeps
  the grill from starting without it.
- The lead's reap closes the grill's pane within one watch cycle of the
  merge, usually before the idle grill notices it, and the shepherd's watch
  runs no member checks in a led herd (D72). A lead note reaches the
  shepherd's watch, which already prints lead mailboxes.
