# D121

## Decision

Amends D114. A credentials lead's sandbox also opens `<config dir>/broker/`
to reads, beside D114's `herds/`, `config.toml`, `trello.env`, `ssh/config`
and `credentials/<herd>.key` (LH-358). The lead adds it to the allows it
gets from the shared set; a sandboxed worker, which has no broker, doesn't.

## Why

- **Its broker lives there.** The lead's own `loopherd` reads the broker's
  lock, stop and restart files and its replies in `broker/` (D98). With the
  whole config dir denied, every `feed`, `tell` and `prompt` from the lead
  would fail as "no broker is running".
- **The whole directory, not the lead's files,** because those are five
  paths that come and go with each `broker-restart`, and `broker/` holds no
  key or token: locks, pids and other leads' replies, which Limits names.
- **The live test confirmed the carve.** A sandboxed credentials lead read
  its own key, herd file, `ssh/config`, `config.toml` and `trello.env` under
  the denied config dir and reached its broker, whose `broker/` was made
  after its Claude started; its own token, other herds' keys and tokens,
  another member's `sandbox/*.json` and `/mnt/c` were hidden.

## Limits

- A lead can read other leads' broker replies, which carry those leads'
  command output, and can flock their lock and restart files.
- A herd whose repo is on a Windows drive gets its own worktree denied;
  nothing refuses it yet.
- A drive mounted or unmounted after a lead starts makes `restart` and
  `revive` warn that its sandbox predates its herd's switches.
