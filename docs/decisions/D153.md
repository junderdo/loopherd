# D153

Supersedes the key choice in D88 and D146.

## Decision

In `loopherd view`, `s` starts or focuses the shepherd, with the behavior D88
gave `S`: a second press while the shepherd comes up does nothing, and `q`
then waits for it. `x` shows or hides strays, the toggle `s` was. `S` is
unbound, with no alias. The footer reads `x strays` and `s shepherd`.

## Why

The human's call, 2026-11-24 (LH-543): a capital `S` for the shepherd beside
a lowercase `s` for strays was confusing, and the shepherd is the key people
need most. `x` stands for the "extra" agents herdr runs outside the ledger.
Dropping `S` outright keeps one key per action.
