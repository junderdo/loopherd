# D153

## Decision

A lead whose worktree lies inside Claude Code's per-uid temp dir
(`$CLAUDE_CODE_TMPDIR`, else the OS temp dir, joined with `claude-<uid>`)
counts as exposed (LH-542). Under `require`, and for a `credentials` lead
under either mode, `lead` refuses it, naming the worktree and the temp dir,
with the fix: move the herd's repo out of it. Under `prefer` it starts, with
a warning in its mailbox. `loopherd doctor` reports it per herd as
`herd <h> sandbox repo`, a failure or warning by the same rule. The live
containment test skips with doctor's line when its checkout is exposed.

## Why

Claude Code's sandbox opens that temp dir to every command's writes, beside
the `allowWrite` loopherd gives it, and no setting closes it. A herd whose
repo sits there, such as an agent's scratchpad checkout, leaves its lead
able to write the other members' worktrees and the main checkout, so the
D89 guarantee that a lead writes only its own worktree doesn't hold. That is
how the 0.2.1 live run failed: the same test passed 8 of 8 from a checkout
under `~`, and failed 2 of 2 from one under `/tmp/claude-1000`, writing the
sibling worktree and the main checkout and nothing else. The check is a
separate finding rather than a settings loosening because its fix is moving
the repo, not removing a key. Workers have the same exposure and are left
to their own card.
