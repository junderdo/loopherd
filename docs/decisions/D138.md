# D138

## Decision

Follow-ups to attended members (D112), decided with the human on LH-393
(2026-11-21). Amends D112's watch and revive bullets and D116's drained rule.

- **Drained.** An open attended member, whatever its state, holds off
  neither `<herd> drained` nor `all drained`, except while its `gone` is
  still to print; a `slot = false` member still does while it may work
  (D116). It already held off no `out of work`.
- **Merge alerts.** A lead's `merge_alert` (D85) skips a pull request whose
  head branch is an attended member's of its herd, retired or not: the
  watch already prints `<name> merged`, and D112 sends nothing through
  `[notify]` for it. The lookup asks gh for `headRefName` too.
- **Return focus.** `retire --close` compares the focused pane with the pane
  it resolved and is closing, not the ledger's `pane_id`, so a moved member
  still sends the human back.
- **Revive.** A `gone` from `pane.closed` now records the closed pane, as
  one from `tab.closed` or `workspace.closed` records its tab or workspace.
  Startup and nameless `revive` skip an attended member only when its last
  entry is such a closed `gone`; one from `pane.exited` or a watch seeing
  the agent vanish (herdr going down) is revived. `revive --name` still
  brings back either.

Limits:

- Whether `herdr server stop` fires `pane.closed`, `tab.closed` or
  `workspace.closed` is still unverified (D66, D69). If it does, an
  attended member is skipped after a stop, as before this.
- A pane the human closed before this change recorded a plain `gone`, so
  such a member is revived once.
- A Claude the human exits leaves its shell, so herdr fires no event;
  a watch's two-poll check records a plain `gone`, and it is revived.
- A lead whose paused herd drains stops with a grill still open, so no
  watch prints that grill's `merged` and nothing reaps it until the lead
  watches again; the shepherd's watch covers unled herds only (LH-484).

## Why

An open grill kept `drained` from printing whenever the human was talking
in it, though the watch reads none of its events. Merge alerts named the
grill's own PR, which the lead's watch already reported. `retire` missed a
focused member whose pane had moved. And every `gone` looked alike to
revive, so a herdr restart left grills closed that the human never closed.
The mailbox can tell a close from a loss once `pane.closed` says so, which
costs one optional field.
