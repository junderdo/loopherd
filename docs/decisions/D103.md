# D103

## Decision

Amends D17: a lead sends only blocking findings back to members, not every
in-scope gap. Review rounds send back only what must change before merge
(LH-325).

- **Only blocking findings fail a round, and only they go back while it
  fails.** Blocking keeps its bar: inside the task's acceptance criteria,
  or a correctness or safety bug the PR introduces. Minors from any round
  (and any other non-blocking finding, whatever its severity) go to one
  follow-up task per PR (with `new_cards` off, listed in a question to the
  human), or, when the member has a next PR in the same card's stack, into
  that PR's scope; they never hold the merge. Nits go
  on the PR as a comment marked non-blocking and are not tasks.
- **A narrower bar after round 1.** A later round gets the previous review
  file and the diff since its head, and answers only: is each previous
  blocking finding fixed, and does the fix diff introduce a new
  correctness or safety bug? A new minor or nit it notices is reported as
  non-blocking and handled as above; it never fails the round.

A triage step in the lead and a member's "won't fix" pushback are deferred
to their own cards. `review_rounds` and its default are unchanged.

## Why

Sending back "any other finding that should be fixed and fits within the
task's scope" made every failing round ship its minors and nits too. The
member wrote code for all of them, and the next round reviewed that code
and found something new in it, often in the minor fixes. `loopherd stats`
over 217 reviewed workers showed a median of 2.5 rounds, 21 past 3 and up
to 11; PR 292's round-2 blocker sat in round 1's fix code, with three more
minors riding along. Holding later rounds to unfixed blockers and new
bugs in the fix diff lets a PR converge.
