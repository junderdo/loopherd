# D171

## Decision

After sending a prompt with `herdr agent prompt`, `spawn` (and so `add`
and `feed`) and `loopherd prompt` confirm it was submitted: within 5s the
agent goes `working`, `blocked` or `done`, or else Claude's input box reads
empty. If neither, they press Enter (`herdr agent send-keys <name> enter`)
and check again, up to 3 times, then fail naming the member and its pane.
The prompt stays marked sent, since its text is in the box and pasting it
again would double it. `tell` keeps its own wait-and-say (D47).

## Why

On 2026-11-23 a fed member sat idle for 6 minutes with its prompt pasted
but not submitted, until a `tell` submitted it. herdr writes the paste and
its Enter as one submission and reports success once both are written,
which doesn't prove Claude took the Enter. Spawn sends that submission the
moment herdr calls a fresh Claude ready, where Claude can read the Enter as
part of the paste. Pressing Enter again submits what the box holds and
never re-pastes it.
