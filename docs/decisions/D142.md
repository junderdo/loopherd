# D142

Amends D32 and D57.

## Decision

A lead's question that waits too long makes that wait visible, rather than
a stalled herd sitting idle.

- **Answered.** When the broker files a lead's `question` report, it writes
  `.awaiting-<pane>` in the mailboxes directory, naming the lead. Any
  prompt submitted in that pane afterwards counts as the answer: a `tell`, a
  raw relay, or the human typing. A prompt that is one `<task-notification>`
  block, as D57 defines it, does not count. `prompt-hook` finds the mark,
  appends an `answered` entry to the lead's mailbox (shown by
  `reports --all`), and removes the mark. An unmarked pane makes no extra
  work, as with D57's label marks. One prompt answers every question
  waiting at that point. A later report from the lead answers nothing.
- **Waiting.** A lead is waiting from its oldest `question` after its last
  `answered` or `start` entry. `loopherd status` and the view show
  `question waiting <age>` on the lead's row.
- **Who notices.** The lead's own watch checks in its 60s round (D72). A
  wait of 30 minutes or more sends a new `[notify]` kind, `waiting`, which
  is in the default `kinds`. The text gives the count, the age and the first
  line of the oldest question. An armed shepherd's watch also prints
  `<lead> waiting <age>`. A `reports/<lead>.waiting` mark, keyed by the
  oldest waiting question, sends it once per stretch across both watches,
  as D127's `no_broker` mark does. An `answered` entry ends the stretch.
  Retiring the lead removes the mark, as for D32's marks. The 30 minutes
  are fixed.
- **No shepherd.** The notification goes to the human whether or not a
  shepherd runs, as D32's lead questions do, so a lead with no shepherd
  still reaches them.
- **Doctor** warns when an unretired lead exists and `[notify]` is off or
  leaves out `waiting`, since then nothing reaches the human.

Only leads are covered: a worker's question goes to its lead, whose own
wait this measures. The shepherd's handoff listing unread reports is LH-481.

LH-482 settled it in a batch grill.

## Why

On 2026-11-23 the loopherd-cards lead filed five gate requests between
17:06 and 17:27 UTC, with all five slots holding PRs behind them. The
shepherd's watch wasn't armed and `[notify]` was off, so nothing read them
until about 22:14. The lead's watch kept running, logging only hourly
`paused` stops. The questions reached the mailbox. What was missing was
anything measuring how long they had waited, and any channel that worked
with `[notify]` off.

- **The lead's watch over the lead re-asking:** the watch runs the whole
  time a lead waits. A re-asked question duplicates the mailbox, notifies
  again under D32, and spends a lead turn on nothing it can act on.
- **Any prompt in the pane over recording `tell`:** `tell` alone misses
  the human typing in the pane and raw relays. Matching a reply to one
  question can't be done reliably. "Heard from no one while waiting" is
  the stall itself.
- **No separate stalled-herd state:** a watch can't tell which slots are
  held by a question. The count and age show the stall.
- **Fixed 30 minutes, once per stretch:** D91's "one switch until a time
  proves wrong". Status and view keep showing the age after the one
  notification.
- **Status and doctor:** the incident ran with `[notify]` off, so a
  notification alone wouldn't have reached anyone.
