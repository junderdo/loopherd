# D45

## Decision

The Stop hook doesn't ask a member to hand off on a stop whose
`background_tasks` include a subagent while its context is at most 1.5x its
limit. The ask comes at the first later stop with no subagent in flight, or
at the first stop past 1.5x the limit. Background shells (a lead's watch)
don't defer it.

## Why

A handoff ends the member's turn and kills its in-flight subagents. A lead
handing off mid-review lost four reviewer runs in one day, each rerun from
scratch. Deferring lets them finish; the 1.5x ceiling bounds how far past
its limit a member that keeps subagents running can drift. Shells are left
out because a lead's watch is always running and would defer every ask.
