# D115

## Decision

Amends D89 and D100 (LH-317). Retiring a lead removes the copies of the
human's config its launch wrote to `<config dir>/sandbox/`:

- **`<lead>.mcp.json` at every retire**, plain or `--close`. The file can
  hold servers' `env` and `headers` secrets. Claude Code loads
  `--mcp-config` servers at startup, and its docs don't say whether it
  rereads the file later; if it does, a running lead loses those servers,
  which takes a capability away rather than loosening its sandbox.
- **`<lead>.json` once its Claude has ended**: when `retire --close` closed
  its tab or found it gone, on the herdr session the lead runs in. A plain
  retire, a close that keeps the tab, or one from another session, which
  can't see the tab, leaves it; a later `retire --close` on the retired
  entry from the lead's session removes it.
- **`<lead>.lead.json` stays.** Guards read it to protect the lead's
  worktree, and `lead` rewrites all three when the name is led again.
- Only an entry whose role is lead, or a role from another loopherd version
  that may be one (D99), and that has a `<lead>.lead.json` has them removed.
  The role check matters because a plain `loopherd spawn`, outside a herd,
  refuses only a name a live agent holds: once lead-x's tab is gone it can
  start a member named lead-x while lead-x's plain retire has left its
  files, and retiring that member must leave them (LH-422). `add` and
  `feed` refuse `lead-*` names outright (D107).
- Only the human's or shepherd's retire of a lead removes them. A lead's
  broker retires only its herd's members, judged from a ledger the lead can
  write, so it leaves these files alone. Feed's reap, including a member's
  reap after its `after_merge`, also runs the retire (`retire_and_close` in
  `src/feed.rs`), but it reaps only members, which aren't leads, so it
  removes no lead's files; a sandboxed worker's own files go as D107 says.

## Why

- **The settings file is what keeps a running lead sandboxed.** Claude Code
  reloads settings files while it runs (D100), so deleting it under a live
  lead could drop its sandbox. It also holds a credentials lead's token,
  which is why a close that ends the lead removes it.
- **A forged entry can't reach another lead's files.** Without the lead
  check, a lead could rewrite another lead's entry as its own member and
  retire it through its broker.
