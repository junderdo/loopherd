# D32

## Decision

A member's ledger session follows Claude Code's `continued-in` records. Claude
can move a conversation to a new session without running a SessionStart hook;
the old transcript then holds a `{"type":"continued-in","continuedInSessionId":…}`
record. `continuation::live_session` resolves a session to its live one: it
reads the transcript, follows its last `continued-in` record to the sibling
`<session>.jsonl` when no `user` or `assistant` record comes after it, and
repeats, stopping at a missing transcript (the last session found wins), a
session already seen, or 32 hops. A session with no transcript resolves to
itself, and a resolved session another live member records is not taken.

- The Stop and SessionStart hooks take the session they're given as resolved,
  and move a member whose recorded session resolves elsewhere to the resolved
  one, unless another live member records it.
- The Stop hook's handoff check measures the live transcript and marks the ask
  under the live session.
- `loopherd revive` resolves both the entry's session and herdr's agents'
  sessions before matching, then resumes and records the resolved one.
- `loopherd restart` resolves the old session before sending `/clear` and
  doesn't take that live one for the cleared session; it records the cleared
  session as resolved.

## Why

On `lead-loopherd-cards` (LH-117) a `/clear` continued into a new session 13s
after its SessionStart hook ran. The hooks kept reporting the old session, so
the ledger pointed at a 16-line stub: the Stop hook measured the stub and never
asked for a handoff at 195k tokens, and revive later resumed an empty session.
What triggers the continuation is unknown, so loopherd follows the record
rather than depending on when it happens. A continued session can also be
resumed later and go on in its own transcript (`e692a17b` on the same herd
has 92 turns after its record), so a record followed by a turn is stale. The record is read from transcripts
because no hook announces it; the continuation lands in the same project
directory, so the chain is followed among siblings.
