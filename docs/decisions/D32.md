# D32

## Decision

`ready-for-agent` and `blocked` are independent labels. A card gets
`ready-for-agent` when it is written fully specified, plus `blocked` while it
waits on other cards. `scripts/trello unblock` only comments and removes
`blocked`; it never adds `ready-for-agent`.

## Why

Adding `ready-for-agent` on unblock (LH-111) made a card that was blocked
before it was fully specified claimable once its blockers finished. A
`blocked` card is never claimable, so carrying `ready-for-agent` while
blocked is already safe, and an under-specified card stays unclaimable after
unblocking until a human specifies it.
