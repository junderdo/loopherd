# D77

## Decision

A member restarts itself after its handoff with `loopherd restart --self`,
run from its own pane. Leads are told to; workers in a led herd keep being
restarted by their lead (D20).

- **Up front**, changing nothing on refusal: the caller's `$HERDR_PANE_ID`
  must be a non-retired ledger member of this herdr, with a handoff file and
  a `handoff` report since its last start and no stop since it, so the
  waiter never takes an earlier turn's `finished` for this one. `--self` takes no name and
  conflicts with `--force`.
- **Waiter.** It then starts `loopherd restart-wait <name>` (hidden) in a
  session of its own (`setsid`, stdio on `/dev/null`) and returns, printing what it
  will do. The waiter polls every 2s and runs the plain restart, never
  forced, once the stop since the handoff reads `finished` with nothing in
  flight and herdr shows the member idle or done. Same pane, worktree,
  gates and ledger entry.
- **Gives up** after 30 minutes, on a start since the handoff that didn't
  use it (a revive), on a failed restart, or on any error, with a
  `self-restart …` note in the member's mailbox naming why. The handoff stays
  for a plain `loopherd restart` unless the restart failed after moving it.
  `restart-wait` refuses to run without the claim `--self` hands it.
- **One waiter per member**: `--self` flocks
  `$XDG_STATE_HOME/loopherd/restarts/<name>.waiter.lock` and hands the open
  file to the waiter, which holds it until it exits; a second `--self`
  refuses while it is held.
- **No double restart**: every restart flocks `restarts/<name>.restart.lock`
  for its run and refuses while another holds it. A waiter that finds it
  held keeps waiting, past its timeout too; one that finds the handoff file
  gone exits without a note, since another restart used it.
- **Gate.** `--self` isn't `--force`, so the PreToolUse hook allows it for a
  lead.

## Why

A lead's handoff otherwise waited on a shepherd session to read its
`handoff` and `finished` lines. D20 refused a restart from the member's own
pane only because the caller is mid-turn when `/clear` goes out; waiting
for its own `finished` stop removes that. Spawning a new pane and closing
the old one was rejected: identity is keyed on `$HERDR_PANE_ID`, the lead's
gate hook wouldn't gate an unrecorded pane, and two sessions would share
one identity until the old pane closed (LH-272).
